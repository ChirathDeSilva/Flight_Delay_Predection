Project guide

About Altura.

A practical machine-learning application for estimating flight delay severity from operational, contextual and weather information.

Project and dataset

The app is trained on the 2024 flight and weather data prepared in the accompanying notebook. It learns patterns from scheduled operations and observed arrival delay outcomes.

Preprocessing

Categorical airline and airport codes are encoded with the saved training encoder. Time values become cyclical features, route distance is calculated from airport coordinates, and missing optional context is filled with stored historical or typical values.

Model

The deployed model is {{ model_name }}, loaded from the saved model bundle with the same feature order, class weights and tuned decision multipliers used during evaluation.

What you can enter

  • Flight date and scheduled departure time
  • Airline, origin and destination airports
  • Optional scheduled arrival and duration
  • Optional origin/destination temperature, precipitation and wind
  • Optional recent delay averages for airports and airline
